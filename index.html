AI-Powered LinkedIn Content Engine

Speak or type raw content into Slack. The system handles everything else.

A Slack message triggers a Notion page update → Notion triggers Gemini to refine the raw content into a publish-ready LinkedIn post → the refined post replaces the raw text in Notion → auto-publishes to LinkedIn → Notion clears itself automatically to reset the loop without re-triggering.

No manual steps. No copy-pasting. No scheduling. The pipeline is self-resetting.
